The pain

You have done the diets. The weight comes back, or it never really left, and you feel tired.

Stubborn weight is often a hormone, blood-sugar, and inflammation story wearing a clothing-size costume. That is why we start with comprehensive bloodwork and body composition, at no cost, before anyone talks about a protocol.

Care is personalized and medically supervised. Some patients use peptide support. Some need hormone optimization first. Some need a diet as disciplined as the one Dr. Spence still walks people through — and a nurse practitioner who will meet them every month when the scale is honest.

The point is not a before-and-after photo. The point is blood sugar, mood, inflammation, and a body that can stay in the life you already have.
