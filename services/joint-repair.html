The pain

Walking, hiking, or getting out of bed hurts. A doctor may have said you need a joint replacement.

Most people do not come here looking for a treatment name. They come because the joint has started to run their calendar. Hiking, fishing, golf, a grandchild on a hip, a night of sleep that ends at 3 a.m. — the life is still there. The joint is in the way.

At Life Enhancement Clinics we use human umbilical cord tissue products, processed in FDA-registered labs and intended for homologous use: to cushion and support soft tissue. The work is done in the office. There is no general anesthesia and no hospital stay.

This is not a promise that cartilage will grow back, or that a future replacement will never be needed. Some patients are better served by orthopedic surgery. The consult exists to find out which side of that line you are on — with labs, imaging, and a straight conversation.
