The pain

You cannot sleep well. You have no energy. Weight is harder to manage. People say this is just aging.

Pellet-based bioidentical hormone replacement is how we restore a steadier signal — without a daily patch, cream, or pill to remember. The hormones match the body's own chemistry. The dose is built from your labs, not a catalog.

Patients come in saying they have lost the person they used to be in the afternoon. They leave the first visit with a plan and, if they are a candidate, a pellet placed in a short in-clinic visit that typically lasts three to six months.

We do not treat a number in isolation. Sleep, body composition, and how you actually feel in a Montana week are part of the same picture.
