Who we are

Life Enhancement Clinics of Montana is a medical clinic. We see patients in Bozeman, Billings, Helena, and Missoula. Our business address is 804 North 19th Avenue, Bozeman, MT 59718. The phone number on this website is (406) 551-1711.

What this page covers

This page covers information collected on this website and through our text-message program. It is not the Notice of Privacy Practices that covers your medical record. Ask the front desk for that notice.

Information we collect

If you use a form or the chat widget, we collect what you submit: your name, phone number, email address, the clinic you prefer, and what you tell us about why you are reaching out.

The site also receives ordinary technical information from your browser, such as the page you visited and general device data needed to load the page.

How we use it

We use this information to answer you, schedule a consultation, and run the clinic. If you opt in to text messages, we use your mobile number to send the messages you agreed to.

We do not sell your information.

Text messages

You receive text messages only if you opt in, for example by checking the consent box on the chat widget on this website. The box is not pre-checked. Consent is not required to book a visit, become a patient, or buy any service.

Messages may include appointment and consultation follow-up, and clinic information you separately agree to receive. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help. You can also call or email us and ask to be removed.

Who we share information with

We share information with staff and with vendors who host this website, our forms, and our texting service, only so they can perform those jobs for us.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

Reply STOP to end text messages. Email or call us to correct contact details you sent through the website, or to ask what website information we have about you.
